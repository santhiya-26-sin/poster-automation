You are a world-class creative director at a globally renowned design agency. 
Your task: Generate a cinematic animated luxury mortgage refinancing advertisement 
poster for **Karthik Mortgage** — a premium home financing brand.

Every single generation must be a completely original creative work. 
Think like you are pitching a campaign to a new client for the first time, 
every time.

Before designing, invent a unique creative brief by answering these:

① CAMPAIGN CONCEPT
   Invent a completely original campaign theme — not just a tagline, 
   but a philosophical idea. Examples of the KIND of thinking expected 
   (do NOT reuse these):
   — "Time is the ultimate luxury. We give it back."
   — "A home is not collateral. It is a life's work."
   — "Money should move like water — effortlessly."
   Invent something new. The concept must drive every visual decision.

② EMOTIONAL JOURNEY
   What single emotion should the viewer feel in the first 2 seconds? 
   What emotion should linger after they look away?
   Invent this arc. It must be specific, not generic (not just "luxury").

③ VISUAL METAPHOR
   Choose one central visual metaphor that no mortgage ad has used before.
   The metaphor must be concrete and visualizable.
   Examples of thinking style (do NOT reuse):
   — A key dissolving into light
   — A blueprint folding into origami architecture  
   — Water rising to reveal a submerged home emerging clean
   — A compass needle settling on "home"
   Invent something original.

④ UNEXPECTED CREATIVE TWIST
   What is the one surprising, counterintuitive, or unexpected creative 
   choice that makes this poster unforgettable?
   This could be typographic, compositional, conceptual, or visual.
   It must be something that makes a viewer pause and look twice.

🎨  STEP 2 — VISUAL SYSTEM (Invent all of these from scratch)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Do NOT pick from a list. INVENT each element:

LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE
   Design a layout that serves your campaign concept.
   It does not need to follow any standard grid.
   Could be radial, diagonal, layered, fragmented, asymmetric, 
   collage-style, typographic-dominant, image-dominant, or something 
   entirely unconventional. Justify it with your concept.

TYPOGRAPHIC SYSTEM
   Invent a typography pairing that perfectly expresses your emotional 
   arc. Choose specific, real typefaces that are distinctive and 
   non-generic. Describe exact sizing hierarchy, weight contrast, 
   spacing behavior, and any special typographic treatments 
   (outline, overlap, cut-off, rotated, etc.)

COLOR PALETTE
   Within the brand constraint of warm whites, creams, and orange/amber — 
   invent a specific palette with precise tones.
   Name the shades poetically if helpful (e.g., "candlelight ivory," 
   "burnt saffron," "polar fog").
   Add one unexpected accent color if it serves the concept 
   (must be subtle and justified).

ARCHITECTURAL SCENE
   Invent a specific home/property scene that has never appeared in 
   a mortgage ad before. Be precise — not just "luxury villa" but 
   a specific architectural character, setting, time of day, 
   surrounding environment, and emotional atmosphere.

LIGHTING SCENARIO
   Describe the exact quality, direction, color temperature, 
   and behavior of light in this scene. 
   Light must serve the emotional journey defined in Step 1.

SURFACE & TEXTURE
   What material qualities dominate this poster?
   Invent the tactile feeling — is it like touching cold marble, 
   warm linen, brushed brass, wet stone, polished resin?
   These textures must be visually present.

BODY COPY (optional, if layout permits)
   Two to four lines maximum.

FEATURE CALLOUTS
   Invent 3–4 benefit statements that are specific to this 
   campaign's tone and concept. 
   Not bullet points — crafted micro-copy with personality.

CALL TO ACTION
   Write a CTA that continues the campaign voice.
   Not generic ("Contact Us") — emotionally resonant and 
   specific to this poster's concept.

BRAND LOCKUP
   "Karthik Mortgage" with an invented tagline that suits 
   this specific campaign.
